# Report Tour

9 pages covering ticket operations, service level, call analytics, agent
performance, and customer analytics for a support organization.

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Landing page: a KPI band (ticket volume, satisfaction, workload snapshot), a total-tickets trend chart, and a service-level gauge against target — the executive at-a-glance view. |
| **Monthly Trends** | Ticket volume and resolution trends over time: a monthly status overview, closed-vs-resolved breakdown, unresolved-status bar, and a combined monthly performance view (volume, resolution efficiency, CSAT). |
| **Ticket Operations** | Active workflow tracking: a real-time ticket-summary card, tickets by priority, ticket distribution by channel, regional workload vs. backlog, and resolution speed (delayed 48h+ vs. resolved within 24h) by category. |
| **Channel Analysis** | Operational metrics per support channel (Email, Phone, Chat, Social Media): volume by priority and one card each for total/resolution/CSAT/escalation, plus each channel's top issue subcategories. |
| **Issue Analysis** | Category and subcategory breakdown: ticket distribution by category, top 5 issue subcategories, a full category/subcategory performance breakdown (volume, resolution time, escalation, CSAT), and reopened-issue volume by category. |
| **Service Level & SLA** | Response-time and SLA tracking: service level vs. target, SLA performance and response times by channel, a service-level trend (MoM/YoY), current-vs-prior-year SLA by subcategory, and response-time-vs-target performance. |
| **Call Analytics** | Phone-call workload: calls by priority and by channel, call share by priority/channel, regional call performance (single- vs. multi-call resolution), and monthly/yearly call-volume comparisons. |
| **Agent Performance** | Individual and team productivity: workload & volume analytics, an agent KPI summary (team-wide satisfaction/resolution/workload), a per-agent operational breakdown, and top-5-agent rankings by call volume and by ticket volume. |
| **Customer Analytics** | Customer-base breakdown: total customers (with YoY comparison), ticket/call load per customer, high-effort case volume, and customer distribution by segment, region, and tier, plus a top-10-customers-by-ticket-volume ranking. |

Every page shares a consistent shell: a bookmarked left-hand navigator, a filter
panel (date range, channel, priority, team/agent, region, status — the specific set
varies by page), and a description flyout explaining how to read that page — all
localized through the dynamic title/label system described in
[Dynamic Titles & Localization](dynamic-titles.md).
